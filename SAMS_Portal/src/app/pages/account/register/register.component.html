2. Platform Description

INFOASSET SAMS is an enterprise-grade asset management platform providing organisations with tools to track, manage, and maintain physical assets across multiple sites and branches. Features include QR & Barcode generation, RFID/GPS tracking, role-based access control (RBAC), asset lifecycle management, supplier management, maintenance scheduling, and comprehensive reporting.

4. Asset Data & Accuracy

You are responsible for the accuracy of all asset data entered into the system. INFOASSET SAMS provides tools and infrastructure; the validity and completeness of asset records, valuations, depreciation schedules, and maintenance logs remain your organisation's responsibility. We are not liable for business decisions made based on asset data you have entered.

5. Subscription & Billing

Access to SAMS is provided on a subscription basis. Pricing depends on your selected plan, number of users, and asset count. Subscriptions are billed in advance. Failure to maintain an active subscription will result in read-only access. Data is retained for 90 days after subscription expiry before permanent deletion.

6. Prohibited Use

You may not use SAMS to store or manage data related to illegal assets or activities; attempt to reverse-engineer or exploit the platform; share access credentials with unauthorised parties; use automated scripts to extract bulk data without prior written approval; or circumvent any security or access control measures.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide during registration (name, email, phone, country), asset and organisational data you enter into SAMS, device and browser information for security purposes (IP address, operating system, browser type), geolocation data when you grant permission, and usage analytics to improve platform performance.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used to operate and improve the SAMS platform, authenticate your identity and manage your session, send transactional emails (OTP verification, account alerts, subscription notices), provide customer support, generate anonymised usage analytics, and comply with legal obligations. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

3. Asset & Organisational Data

All asset records, user data, site configurations, and reports you create in SAMS belong to your organisation. We act as a data processor on your behalf. This data is stored in encrypted form and is never used for commercial profiling, shared with competitors, or accessed by INFOASSET staff without your explicit consent or a legal requirement.

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including AES-256 encryption at rest, TLS 1.3 encryption in transit, role-based access controls, multi-factor authentication options, regular security audits, and automated intrusion detection. Your passwords are hashed using bcrypt and are never stored in plain text.

5. Cookies & Tracking

SAMS uses essential session cookies required for authentication and security. We use analytics cookies (with your consent) to understand feature usage patterns. No advertising or third-party tracking cookies are used. You can manage cookie preferences in your account settings.

6. Data Retention

Active account data is retained for the duration of your subscription. Deleted records are soft-deleted and permanently purged after 30 days. Upon account closure, all data is retained for 90 days to allow export, then permanently deleted. Audit logs are retained for 12 months for compliance purposes.

7. Your Rights (GDPR & PDPA)

You have the right to access, rectify, or export your personal data at any time from account settings. You may request account deletion, object to data processing, and request data portability.
